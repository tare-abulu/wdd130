Delta State

Delta State is located in the South-South region of Nigeria. It is not only home to the Ijaw, Itsekiri, and Urhobo tribes, but it is also a place where people from diverse cultural backgrounds live and work together. The state is known for its rich cultural heritage, diverse population, and important role in Nigeria's oil and gas industry.
